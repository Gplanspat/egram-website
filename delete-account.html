Delete Your eGram Account

To request deletion of your eGram account and associated data:

  1. Open the eGram app.
  2. Go to Settings → Delete Account.
  3. Confirm your request.

Or email: support@egramapp.com

Data deleted

  • Profile information
  • Student/Parent account
  • Leaderboard records
  • Learning progress

Some financial or legal records may be retained for up to 90 days where required by law.
